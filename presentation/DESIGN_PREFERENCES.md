# Presentation design preferences

Apply these defaults to future presentations in this repository.

- Use **Source Sans Pro** for all presentation text.
- Keep all slide content inside a **0.9 cm safe margin** on each side; do not place labels, diagrams, page numbers, or decorative marks at the slide edge.
- Use the **Springer Nature Elements** palette: deep blue `#01324B` as the primary dark colour, blue `#0070A8` for structure, teal `#00A69D` and orange `#F58220` as restrained accents, with neutral greys for background and secondary text.
- Prefer a restrained, editorial visual language: clear hierarchy, mostly flat shapes, generous whitespace, and no decorative gradients or excessive rounded cards.
- Do a visual render check after substantive layout changes; compilation success alone is not sufficient.

Palette source: Springer Nature Elements Design System, https://eds.springernature.com/design-tokens/springernature/palette/
